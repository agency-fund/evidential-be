Before anything else

Your children's data stays yours

Evidential connects to the warehouse you already have and runs analysis there. It keeps only what it needs to run an experiment.

Stays with you

  • All raw participant data, in your own warehouse (BigQuery, Redshift, or Postgres).
  • Analysis queries run directly in your database. Evidential does not copy your tables.
  • The option to host Evidential yourself. It is free and open source.

What Evidential keeps

  • Experiment setups: name, hypothesis, dates, arms, and metrics.
  • Summary results, saved as snapshots over time.
  • The participant IDs you use, to record who got which arm and their outcome.
  • For personalized experiments, the context values your app sends (for example, "has a smartphone").
  • Distinct values of columns you filter on, so filters can autocomplete.

Your part

  • Use participant IDs that contain no personal information, or hash them first.
  • Don't filter on columns that hold personal details such as names or phone numbers.
  • Send only non-identifying context values to personalized experiments.

Source: Evidential's data storage documentation (docs.evidential.dev, Analytics › Data Storage) and the data the Evidential server stores for each experiment.

Why run more than one

One experiment, or a system of them

The same iron program, run two ways. Students in the same school are alike (an intraclass correlation of 0.15), which Evidential's power check accounts for.

Without a pilot
  1. Launch the iron program in 30 schools. 1,500 students looks like plenty.
  2. Because students within a school are alike, those 1,500 behave like about 180 independent students.
  3. The trial can only reliably detect gains of about 20%.

A real 14% gain most likely comes back "inconclusive", after a full year and budget.

With Evidential, step by step
  1. Run a 20-school pilot. SMS reminders alone show no clear effect, and the pilot measures the school-level correlation.
  2. Redesign: add in-person peer champions, and let the power check size the trial: 88 schools to detect a 12% gain.
  3. Run Iron Support in 90 schools with uneven arms (20 / 40 / 40).

Peer champions raise adherence 14%, with a clear result to scale.

Year 1: testFind out what works. Glasses, Iron Pilot, Iron Support, Health Report.
Year 2: optimizePick the best version while protecting participants. Handwashing, Dental Kit.
Year 3: personalizeGive each family what works for them. Nutrition.

Getting started

Start with one question you already have

Connect your warehouse, describe who takes part and what you want to change, and Evidential checks whether you have enough people before you spend anything.

  • Bandits are not scary. They start even and send more people to what is working.
  • Context matters. The best option for one family can be the worst for another.
  • Every experiment makes the next one smarter. Pilots size trials; winners become options.
  • You don't need a data team. Power checks, balance, and analysis are built in.
